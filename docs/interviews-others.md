# Interviews: people who tried a health app and stopped (30 Oct 2026, WhatsApp questionnaire)

## Respondent A
1. Apps: HealthifyMe
2. Used it for a week and stopped.
3. Why: "too much effort to log, it wasn't accurate, forgot about it in the end."
4. Moment: "just found it boring to track everyday."
5. Liked: "it did the job well and alrdy had some common Indian diets included."
6. Food tracking hard: "logging and getting the exact calorie count accuracy."
7. Never paid; would not.
8. (input preference: not answered)
9. "reminders r good, but sometimes when I'm occupied I forget abt it"
10. One fix: "Make everything easy for me maybe, remove the boring work like manual logging. I've seen apps where people click pics of their meal and ask AI to tell the calorie count, but rn I don't really care… maybe one day when I decide to be healthy and committed I might just use any app."

## Respondent B
1. Apps: Google Fit, MyFitnessPal, Strava, Noise smartwatch app, Increase Height Workout, Lose Weight App for Men, Home Workout – No Equipment, Six Pack in 30 Days (last four by Leap Fitness Group).
2. Duration: Noise app while he had the watch; Strava weekly for runs; Increase Height Workout 1 month; Lose Weight App for Men a few weeks; Home Workout 7 months; Google Fit 2–4 months.
3. Why stopped: got bored of Google Fit, Noise, MyFitnessPal; Increase Height and Lose Weight "wasn't that effective and got bored early"; Six Pack in 30 Days "was effective and reached goal."
4. Moment: "When they ask for premium, got bored, ads."
5. Liked: interface was great; best and most practical was Six Pack in 30 Days.
6. Food tracking: "tried for 2 weeks but it is very hectic and boring also has effect after a considerable amount of time so got bored."
7. Never paid: "We get advice from people around us… no need as such." Max ₹100/month.
8. "If it understands voice note then greattt! That would be efficient, engaging, will give reality check, fun, and best way to keep records!"
9. "Message me first, make widgets, keep me tracking!"
10. "Engagement issues with customers, be flexible with providing diet, 2 free consultation by dietician, should know Indian vegetables, recipes, for vegetarians mainly!"
