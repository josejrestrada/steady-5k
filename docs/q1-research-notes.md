## Published sources
1. Kidman PG, Curtis RG, Watson A, Maher CA. When and Why Adults Abandon Lifestyle Behavior and Mental Health Mobile Apps: Scoping Review. J Med Internet Res 2024;26:e56897. doi:10.2196/56897. Median 70% of users stop within 100 days; 22 reasons in 6 categories incl. tedious data entry, hidden costs, lack of personalisation, annoying notifications. Cites 69% of fitness apps abandoned within 90 days.
2. Cordeiro F, Epstein DA, Thomaz E, et al. Barriers and Negative Nudges: Exploring Challenges in Food Journaling. CHI 2015. Survey of 141 food loggers + 5,526 forum posts (MyFitnessPal, FatSecret, CalorieCount). Logging effort is the main barrier; packaged food easy to log (6.5/7), home/party food hard (~3/7); users changed what they ate to make logging easier.
